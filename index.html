Answers & Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about offline photo puzzles, privacy, Siri Shortcuts, and multiplayer challenges.

Can I turn my own iPhone camera roll photos into custom jigsaw puzzles offline?

Yes. JigShare converts any photo from your iPhone or iPad library into a custom Pop Art jigsaw puzzle from 2x2 up to 6x6 grids completely on-device, working 100% offline in Guest Mode with no account registration required.

Is JigShare free to play and are there any subscriptions or paywalls?

JigShare is 100% free to download and play with zero subscriptions or In-App Purchases. Players earn free daily skill points by solving puzzles, with optional user-initiated rewarded videos if extra hint tools are desired.

How do multiplayer photo puzzle challenges work with friends?

You can package one photo or a multi-level gauntlet with custom grid sizes and 180-degree piece rotation into a challenge link or portable .jig file so friends can race against your exact completion time.

What is the Daily Pop Puzzle and Polaroid victory card feature?

Every day JigShare generates a deterministic Pop Art puzzle (3x3 on weekdays, 4x4 on weekends) tracked on a 14-day streak calendar, and lets you export a 3x Retina retro Polaroid card stamped with your finish time and achievement badge.

Are my personal photos uploaded to the cloud when I play solo?

No. Solo puzzles and Polaroid victory cards are processed and stored strictly on your device. Images are only uploaded when you explicitly send a cloud challenge to friends, screened by AI SafeSearch and automatically deleted after 7 days.

Which devices, Apple Shortcuts, and languages does JigShare support?

JigShare runs natively on iPhone, iPad, and unfolded iPhone Duo (iOS 17.0+), integrates with Siri Shortcuts and Spotlight search, and ships with full localization in 9 languages: English, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, and Portuguese.
